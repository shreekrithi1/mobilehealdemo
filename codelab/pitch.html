04 / TRUST & CONTROL

Confidence you can inspect.
Gates you cannot skip.

Evidence confidence measures coverage. It is not a probability of correctness.

Acceptance coverage30
Executed verification30
Required reviews25
Provenance15

Threshold: 85 / 100. Missing, skipped, unavailable or stale evidence earns zero.

MANDATORY BLOCKERS

A high score never overrides:

  • A failed required check or critical finding
  • A missing reviewer or stale artifact
  • An unauthorized action
  • Missing current human approval
  • An unreconciled mutation outcome

Bounded tools, two rework rounds, persistent receipts and explicit cancellation keep runs traceable.

Deterministic reviewers inspect a declared evidence scope. Semantic, security and device certification require separate verification.
